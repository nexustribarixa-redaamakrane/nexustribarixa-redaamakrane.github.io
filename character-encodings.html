Character Encodings

Character Encodings & Custom Tables

Technical documentation and headers covering SuperUnicode (SUCS) custom codepoint maps, character normalization pipelines, and FontForge encoding tables (.enc) maintained by Nexus Tribarixa. These are proprietary OpenWindows encoding formats, not Unicode PUA.

Key Components

  • SUTF-8 variable byte packing specifications for SuperUnicode codepoints (custom format, not UTF-8).
  • Provisional and permanent gap maps for Plane 0 (BMP), Plane 15 (SPUA-A), and Plane 16 (SPUA-B).
  • Integration with FontForge and fontTools glyph lookup indexing.
